# Book Report: _The Treacherous Path_

**Author:** Vladimir Yakunin  
**Title:** _The Treacherous Path_  
**Genre:** Memoir / Political History  
**Published:** 2018

## Introduction

_The Treacherous Path_ by Vladimir Yakunin is a memoir that describes his experiences during some of the most important political and social changes in modern Russian history. Yakunin discusses his life and career during the final years of the Soviet Union, the collapse of the Soviet system, and the development of modern Russia. Rather than simply describing historical events, Yakunin presents them from his own personal perspective and explains how he believes these changes affected Russia and its people.

## Summary

The book follows Yakunin's experiences as he became involved in Russian public life and eventually rose to become an important figure in the country's political and economic system. A major part of the book focuses on the collapse of the Soviet Union and the difficult period that followed.

Yakunin describes the 1990s as a particularly chaotic and uncertain period. Russia experienced major economic and political changes, while many people struggled with poverty, instability and the rapid transformation from a centrally planned Soviet economy to a market-based system. Yakunin presents this period as a time when Russia lost much of its stability and when powerful individuals gained enormous influence.

The book then moves towards the emergence of Vladimir Putin and the changes that took place under his leadership. Yakunin portrays this period as one in which Russia began to regain stability and rebuild its influence. He also discusses his own career and his experiences working within the Russian government and state-owned institutions.

Throughout the book, Yakunin connects his personal experiences with his broader views about Russia's history, government and place in the world.

## Main Themes

One of the main themes of _The Treacherous Path_ is **change**. Yakunin describes Russia going through enormous transformations within a relatively short period of time. The transition from the Soviet Union to modern Russia was not simple, and the author emphasises the uncertainty and difficulties that accompanied it.

Another important theme is **leadership**. Yakunin argues that strong and effective leadership is important for maintaining stability within a country. His discussion of Vladimir Putin is particularly important because he presents Putin's leadership as a major factor in Russia's recovery following the instability of the 1990s.

The book also deals with **national identity**. Yakunin considers what Russia should be as a country and how it should interact with other nations. He is critical of some Western attitudes towards Russia and argues that Russia has its own political and cultural identity that should not simply be replaced by Western ideas.

A final major theme is **power**. Yakunin describes the competition between different political and economic groups and the struggle to determine Russia's future. His own position within the Russian political establishment gives him a personal perspective on these events.

## The Author's Perspective

Because the book is a memoir, everything is presented through Yakunin's personal perspective. This makes the book interesting because he was directly involved in many of the events he describes. His experiences provide an insider's view of Russian politics and government.

However, this also means that the book should not necessarily be treated as an entirely objective historical account. Yakunin clearly has strong opinions about Russia, the Soviet Union, the West and Vladimir Putin. His positive portrayal of certain political developments reflects his own beliefs and experiences.

## My Opinion

I found _The Treacherous Path_ interesting because it provides a personal perspective on a period of history that had an enormous impact on Russia and the wider world. Instead of simply listing historical events, Yakunin explains how he experienced them and how they influenced his views.

The strongest part of the book is its discussion of the transition from the Soviet Union to modern Russia. It shows how complicated this period was and how quickly political and economic conditions could change.

At times, however, the author's strong political opinions make it difficult to separate his personal interpretation from the historical events themselves. This does not necessarily make the book uninteresting, but it means the reader should remember that it represents Yakunin's point of view.

## Conclusion

Overall, _The Treacherous Path_ is an interesting memoir about Russia's journey from the Soviet Union to the modern era. Vladimir Yakunin uses his own experiences to discuss political change, economic instability, leadership, national identity and Russia's relationship with the rest of the world.

The book is particularly valuable for showing how major historical events can look from the perspective of someone who was personally involved in them. Although Yakunin's opinions and political position clearly influence his interpretation of events, _The Treacherous Path_ provides an engaging account of a complicated and turbulent period in Russian history.
