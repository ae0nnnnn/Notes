## Computers in the Workplace

Computers have allowed employees to: 

1. Access modern productivity tools and specialized software.
2. Carry out their daily work activates efficiently 
3. Easily communicate and collaborate with one another 


In other sectors the use of computers has increased for example the manufacturing sector automation has nearly removed all of its human workers this is because machines can work for longer as well as be more accurate and reliable.

Robotics has also shifted skill sets needed in many sectors, using our factory example again the might have had many lower-skilled workers, but now they would have a smaller set of highly skilled technicians to run and support and monitor the robotic systems.

Lower skilled jobs are quickly becoming automated because of technology for example supermarket checkouts , Airport and hotel checking system and online banking.


However, there also cons for example, staff would get replaced as their jobs are replaced by automation.

## Automated Decision-Making 

This is defined as - "Computers making decisions and taking action based on sets of predefined rules and parameters without any human involvement"

An example of this would be the stock market, as before it was a manual process between the buyer and the seller, but now 90 percent of transactions are carried out automatically via a process called automatic trading.

Computers would follow algorithms check prices and compare values against predetermined parameters to purchase and sell stocks and shares.
