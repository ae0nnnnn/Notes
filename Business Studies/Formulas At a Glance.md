# Formulas at a Glance 

## Gearing 

$$\text{Gearing Ratio} = \left( \frac{\text{Long-Term Liabilities}}{\text{Capital Employed}} \right) \times 100$$

Less than 30% is Low Geared
30% - 50%  is  Average Gearing 
More than 50% is High Gearing 


## Capital Employed

$$\text{Capital Employed} = \text{Non-Current Liabilities} + \text{Shareholder's Funds (Equity)}$$




## Net Profit Margin (NPM):
  $$\text{Net Profit Margin (\%)} = \frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Total Revenue}} \times 100$$

## Gross Profit Margin (GPM):
  $$\text{Gross Profit Margin (\%)} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Total Revenue}} \times 100$$

## Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):
  $$\text{ROCE (\%)} = \frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Capital Employed}} \times 100$$

The Higher % the better 
Watch for the trend over time 
Watch out for low quality profits which boosts ROCE
Leased equipment don't get included in capital employed

Improving ROCE:

Reducing Capital Employed e.g. repaying debt

Raise operating profits e.g. reduce supplier costs 
- Consider the impact on quality 


Reasons for Low ROCE
Capital Investment 
Economic Downturn
Advertising Campaign

## Labour Turnover 

$$\text{Labour Turnover Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Number of Employees Who Left}}{\text{Average Number of Employees}} \right) \times 100$$
