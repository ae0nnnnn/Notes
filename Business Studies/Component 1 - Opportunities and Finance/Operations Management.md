# Operations Management 

## Economies of Scale 

Economies of Scale can be defined as a business grows in size, the average costs per unit fall or the reduction in average cost of production that occur as businesses increases it scale of production.

### Internal Economies of Scale 

Purchasing Economies - This occurs when the business buys goods in bulk and benefits from discounts.

Technical Economies of Scale - Occurs when a business invests in new technology and is able to increase production. As a result production cost per unit falls

Managerial Economies of Scale - Occurs when a large firm can employ specialist workers to complete tasks and spread the costs.

Marketing Economies - Occurs when a business grows and the average cost of advertising per unit falls.

Risk Bearing - When a business purchases a range of products.

Financial - Occurs when a large business can borrow money at a lower rate of interest  than a smaller business.
