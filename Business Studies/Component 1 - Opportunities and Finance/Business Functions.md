
## Departments 

When as a business has traditional structure it will normally structured by functional departments  i.e. departments with clearly defined roles and responsibilities.

This helps with communication as people will know who to speak to if a problem arises and helps workers understand their roles.

In most large companies, the functional areas are as follows 

1. Marketing (Sales and Marketing)
2. Human Resources (Personnel)
3. Finance (Accounts)
4. Operations (production)


The Human Resources Department is responsible for making sure that the departments have enough workers with the right skills.

The finance department ensures that there is money available to carry out activities across all the the departments.

Marketing work with production by providing information about the products that customers want.

Purchasing has the job of making sure that there are enough materials for production to manufacture.

### Role of the Finance Department

The finance department manages a business's money overseeing activities like raising capital, budgeting and playing staff and suppliers.

It is responsible for tracking income and expenditure preparing financial statement to report performance.

This function is crucial for financial health and supports decision making across the other departments to ensure business objectives are met.

Sourcing finance raising capital to fund start up fees or ongoing operations.

Financial record-keeping and preparing and maintaining financial accounts and statements to show the business's financial health and performance.

Financial analysis: analyzing financial performance to identify trends support decision making.

Paying bills and managing payments to suppliers and other creditors.



## Budgeting

A budget is a forward financial plan over a specified period of time.

Budgets are used to measure expected levels of revenue and expenditure.

Large firms will establish budgets or individual  departments such as marketing or human resources.

Examples include Income, Expenditure and profit budgets.

Budgets have many uses in management  such as 
- Establishing priorities and set targets.
- Turn objectives into practical reality.
- Provide direction and coordination
- Assign responsibilities
- Allocate resources 
- Communicate  targets.

Terminology that would be helpful to know -
-  Budgets holder - a person who is accountable for seeing that a business is kept to
- Delegated Budgets - giving some control in the setting of budgets to departments or individuals.
- Profits budget - The target for profit for the business over a given time period 
- Income budget - The sales revenue target for a department or the whole business
- Expenditure Budget - A fixed sum of money to be spent in a given time by a department

### Types of Budgets 

Historical Budgeting 
- Use last year's figures as the basis for the budget
- Realistic in that its based on actual results
- However circumstances may have changed (e.g new products lost customers)
- Does not encourage efficiency 

Zero Budgets 
1. Budget cost and revenues are set zero 
2. Budget is based on new proposals for sales and costs 
3. However circumstances may be changed (e.g new products lost customers credit crunch)
4. Does not encourage efficiency 

### Budgetary Control 

Budgets are monitored to check for any differences of revenue or expensive or expenditure from the budgeted figure.

Any difference is called a variance.

It is the job of the budget holder to monitor any differences and report on them.

Business need to know this information do that they can react appropriately e.g. maybe cut costs.

Budgets variances can be favorable or adverse.

The consider the differences between planned (budgeted) revenue and expenditure levels and actual revenue and expenditure.

Favorable
- Revenue is higher than expected 
- Expenditure is lower that expected 

Adverse 
- Revenue is lower than expected 
- Expenditure is higher than expected 

#### Analyzing Variance 

Business should not investigate every variance as this wastes time instead managers should focus on.

1. The Size of the variance.
2. Whether is a one-off or part of a serious trend 
3. Whether it was predictable 


While budgeting offers benefits there are problems 

Time: Preparation is significant, management time spent on budgeting could potentially be better spent somewhere else.

Worthlessness: The budget is virtually worthless without relevant data, proper planning and accurate implementation.

Dynamic Environment: rapidly changing markets mean that budgets might not be accurate 100% of the time as they haven't accounted for every unforeseen situations.

Impact of Success: The ultimate success of budgeting depends on factors like the importance of financial management.

Marketing is far more than simply advertising and the selling of products and services

It's primarily focused upon the satisfaction of consumer wants and needs and because these are always changing so marketing never stands still either.

Marketing Activities 
Reaching the market
Market analysis
Establishing Marketing objectives
Devising marketing strategies Based around the 4P’s

These are just a small sample of the range of marking activities that aim to provide goods and services that will satfisy customer needs and generate a profit for the business 

### The Importance of Orientation

A business will have to make a decision about what they wish to focus their attraction upon as part of their business activities.

Do they focus upon the product and the production process with the intention of creating technical proficiency that they can offer to the customer.

Or do they focus on meeting the demands of the customer and produce goods and services that they want?

### Types of Orientation 

### Product Orientation 

Product orientated firms focus their efforts upon creating and developing a technically advance product before then trying to sell to the consumer  

Advantages

- The business is able focus upon their own strengths
- Customers are likely to demonstrate brand loyalty due to the quality and uniqueness of the product offering 
- Its allows a business to develop a reputation for innovation and quality as they are focused upon product development
