Command Word = **Analyse**

Approximate breakdown of marks:

AO1 - 2 Marks
AO2  - 2 Marks

### Format of the Question

Context + Question :

Exemplar Question : Analyse one way in which a rise in income could affect demand for BMW cars. (6)

Format of Question : 

Analyse **One** way → (Concept) → (END Point)

### Simple Tech for 6 Markers 

You only need one argument.
But It must include a clear chain.
Try and use the context into every point.


Advisable Sentence structure to adopt :

Vegan BLT - Because, Leading to, Therefore (Its Vegan because most times you can drop the Because)

And secondly there is Leads to, Which Causes, Resulting in

### Simple Tech for 9 Markers

Use a 3 paragraph approach 

1: Yes Point → Explain → Context → Link back to outcome in question.

2: No Point → Explain → Context → Link back to outcome in question.   

3: Conclusion → Yes (or No) → WHY → BUT/ HOWEVER/IT DEPENDS ON →SO 

In More depth 

Paragraph 1: 

1. Make a clear argument supporting the statement 
2. Explain how it works (leads to → which causes → resulting in) Don't stop at one step → build a chain of analysis!
3. Add context (from the case study)
4. Link it to the outcome and explain why this supports statement (e.g. why profits may increase for Volt)

Paragraph 2: 
1. Make a clear argument against the statement 
2. Explain how it works (leads to → which causes → resulting in) Don't stop at one step → build a chain of analysis!
3. Add context (from the case study)
4.  Link it to the outcome and explain why this challenges statement (e.g. why profits may not increase for Volt)

Paragraph 3: 
1. This is the conclusion/judgement 
2. In reality, there is no correctly answer
3. The strongest answers should make a **conditional judgement** to achieve top marks.


####  Conditional Judgement Technique  and Tips

- Yes (or No) - answer the question 
- WHY - Strongest earlier argument developed with context (not just repeating original points).
- But - Introduce the condition - The It depends on 
- So - Explain how that condition affects the outcome and still gives your final judgement.



Keep your final judgement consistent with your opening answer.

For example: say "yes" at the start (in Yes) → still say "yes" at the end (in SO), but now that yes based on the condition set in (BUT)

If you don't include a "But", won't reach the top level.
